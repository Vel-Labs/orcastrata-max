# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

We welcome respectful, constructive participation. Be clear, patient, and
specific. Critique ideas and changes, not people. Respect privacy and different
levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate
intimidation, and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns through the repository's private vulnerability
reporting route when a private channel is required. Do not disclose sensitive
details in a public issue.

Vel Labs maintainers can edit or remove contributions and restrict
participation when conduct harms the project or its community.
